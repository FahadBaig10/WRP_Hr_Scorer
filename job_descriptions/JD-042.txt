Job ID: JD-042
Title: AI Engineer
Location: Karachi (Hybrid)
Experience Level: 2-4 years

About the Role:
We are hiring an AI Engineer to build and maintain machine learning
services that power our internal products. You will work across the full
lifecycle — from prototyping models to deploying reliable, production-grade
inference services. We value clean engineering, sound judgment, and the
ability to ship small, complete features rather than large unfinished ones.

Responsibilities:
- Design and build backend AI services in Python.
- Integrate large language models into applications, including locally
  hosted open-source models.
- Write clean, well-tested, maintainable code with clear documentation.
- Work with databases (SQL) to store and retrieve application data.
- Handle unreliable model output safely: validation, parsing, and graceful
  failure handling.
- Collaborate with the team to scope features and explain technical
  trade-offs.

Required Skills:
- Strong Python (3.10+), including virtual environments and packaging.
- Practical experience with at least one ML or LLM framework.
- Experience working with REST APIs and/or command-line tools.
- Familiarity with SQL databases (SQLite, PostgreSQL, or similar).
- Comfortable with Git and standard version-control workflows.

Nice to Have:
- Experience running local LLMs (Ollama, llama.cpp, or similar).
- Exposure to retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) or vector databases.
- Unit testing experience (pytest).
- Familiarity with data validation libraries (e.g. Pydantic).

What We Value:
- Clear reasoning and reliable, well-structured output.
- The ability to scope work sensibly and defend design decisions.
- A bias toward simple, complete solutions over feature breadth.
